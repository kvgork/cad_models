# v1: Raptor cord finger (GRIP-9020), concept C5 made printable

X1, CAD-checked, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.**

This is the e-NABLE Raptor principle on the tester dovetail:
- three pin joints;
- a flexor cord on the palm side that curls the finger;
- an elastic cord on the back that opens it again.

**Hand first:** the cord runs down the palm side and out under the cord-guide bar to a pull ring. **Servo later:** the SCS0009 slides into the cradle between the fork posts, the spool goes on its spline, and the cord winds onto the spool. Nothing else changes.

Picked by the user on 2026-11-22 ("working prototypes, C5 + C6 only") from the massing [concepts](../concepts/README.md). C6 is [v2_rod-fourbar](../v2_rod-fourbar/README.md).

## Spec

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Finger | 18 wide × 16 thick; joint spacing 45 / 25 / 22 mm (MCP→PIP→DIP→tip, 92 mm). The tip is 152.5 mm above the dovetail bottom. |
| Knuckles | Fork and tongue: the tongue is 8 wide, with 0.3 side gaps and 0.4 radial gap around rounded R8 ends. The palm side is cleared up to R8.4 (the crease), so each joint flexes about 90° before the palm corners meet. |
| Extension stop | A spur on each tongue (sector R10.6) meets a pocket wall in the parent, 2° past straight. At the MCP it meets a stop block between the base cheeks. The elastic holds the finger against these stops. |
| Range (CAD, joint.find_limits) | MCP −2.0 … 114.4°, PIP −2.0 … 92.8°, DIP −2.0 … 92.8° |
| Pins | One-piece snap pins, Ø3.1 with 2 flats, in Ø3.4 teardrop holes (0.15 per side). The heads stand proud on the −Y side, 0.1 off the face. MCP pin 27.9 long, PIP/DIP pins 21.3. |
| Cord and elastic | 2.2 mm square channels: the cord at x −5.5 (palm), the elastic at x +5.5 (back), both through all three phalanges and out at the tip. Moment arm at the joints ≈ 5.5 mm. |
| Base | GRIP-0002 dovetail (same numbers, checked), **moved to y 11.38** so that it sits under the middle of the footprint. Everything past the head has a 45° underside starting at z 6.2, 0.2 above the tester rails, so the base prints without supports and clears the rails and the lock screw. |
| Servo cradle | SCS0009 output along −Y at z 34.5, with 0.5 around the case. Its tabs sit on the cradle face, with 2 Ø1.6 pilot holes for the supplied screws. The fork cheeks start at z 46.2, above the tab tips (45.05), on posts at \|x\| ≥ 6.6, so the servo (case \|x\| ≤ 6.05) slides in along +Y without taking the finger off. |
| Spool (servo stage) | Ø12 drum between Ø18 flanges, centred under the finger. Ø4.1 bore over the Ø4.0 spline: drawn 0.1 over, because small vertical holes print 0.1–0.2 small, so it grips. M2 screw into the spline core. |

## Build plan (hand stage)

Each phalanx goes on with its pin straight away, so nothing loose waits for a pin. Push each pin in from the −Y side (head side) until the barb clicks out on the other side.

| Step | Picture | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ![1](docs/build/step1_base.png) | Slide the base (GRIP-0140) onto the tester dovetail and lock the thumb screw. The finger can also go on first and the base slide on afterwards. |
| 2 | ![2](docs/build/step2_proximal.png) | Put the proximal's round root (GRIP-0141, longest) between the base cheeks. Push the MCP pin (GRIP-0144, the long one) through the cheek, the proximal and the other cheek. |
| 3 | ![3](docs/build/step3_middle.png) | Put the middle's tongue (GRIP-0142) into the proximal's fork and push in a knuckle pin (GRIP-0145). |
| 4 | ![4](docs/build/step4_distal.png) | Put the distal (GRIP-0143) into the middle's fork and push in the second knuckle pin. Every joint should now swing freely from straight to about 90°. |
| 5 | ![5](docs/build/step5_ring.png) | **Cords.** Flexor: knot the Dyneema at the tip, then thread it down the palm channels (x −5.5) of distal, middle and proximal. Run it down the palm side, out under the cord-guide bar, and tie it to the pull ring (GRIP-0146). Elastic: knot it at the tip, thread it down the back channels, loop it round the elastic-anchor bar under the cheeks, and tie it with light tension so the finger rests straight on its stops. |
| 6 | ![6](docs/build/step6_done_front.png) | Done. Pull the ring and the finger curls; let go and the elastic straightens it. |
| — | ![7](docs/build/step7_curled.png) | A curled pose, for reference. |

**Servo stage** (later): slide the SCS0009 in along +Y between the fork posts until its tabs sit on the cradle face, and fix it with its two screws. Push the spool (GRIP-0147) onto the spline and fix it with an M2×10 screw. Take the cord off the ring, tie it through the spool's cord hole and wind it on. The joint stops stay the same.

## Not checked

- **Cord and elastic.** The channels are straight cuts and are not modelled as cords. Not checked: friction, the cord path over the joints at large flexion, the cord running from the proximal to the spool or to the guide bar, the elastic stretch (about 20 mm at full curl), and whether the elastic beats the cord's friction to open the finger.
- **Forces.** Grip force from a hand pull, and from the servo (0.226 N·m stall on a 6 mm spool ≈ 37 N cord pull, before friction), are hand calculations only.
- **Print fits.** Snap pins in the horizontal teardrops (barb 0.3 per side), the spool press fit on the 20T spline, the pins turning freely, the 0.3 side gaps on the knuckles. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.**
- **On the real baseplate.** The dovetail numbers equal GRIP-0002's and the base clears the rail height. Not checked against the real baseplate: the lock screw head, and the board bosses on the plate when the finger is centred 11.4 mm off the slot axis.
- **Spool screw length** (M2×10) and the servo tab screws: NEEDS CHECK.
- **Servo motion:** the cord wrap on the spool over the 300° servo range is not modelled.
