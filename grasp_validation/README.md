# grasp_validation: grasp test bench, build stages 1–2 (GRIP-9015)

These are the first two build stages of the test hardware for the gripper-agnostic grasp validation suite. The design and its sources are in the vault: [`05-Wiki/research/2026-11-15-validating-grasping-performance.md`](/home/koen/Documents/Vaults/Local/05-Wiki/research/2026-11-15-validating-grasping-performance.md), §11. The full spec (fixtures FC-1, DR-1, IO-1) is in that session's `PROTOCOL.md` §4.

- **Stage 1, force chain (FC-1).** A sensor hub box and a calibration kit. Dead weights are the force reference; FSRs and servo current are never the absolute reference.
- **Stage 2, first grasp-force data.** A DR-1a dovetail receptacle (same channel as GRIP-0069, takes any gripper on a GRIP-0002 adapter) and the IO-1 split artifacts in the 30 mm size, as a pinch block (W30) and a wrap cylinder (D30).

First measurement this enables: S01, the grasp-force curve, on the stock SO-101 gripper and on GRIP-9010 finger A. Next stages, not in this folder: the puller PR-1 (stage 3), the test stand TS-1 with drop table and grid plate plus the printable object set OS-1 (stage 4), then the camera and sync logger.

## How the split artifact works

The **load cell** is a TAL220B-format bar: 55 × 12.7 × 12.7 mm, 2 × M5 at 40 mm pitch, rated output 1.0 mV/V, non-linearity and hysteresis ±0.05 %FS, creep ±0.1 %FS over 3 min, safe overload 120 %FS ([HTC TAL220B sheet via Gotron](https://gotron.be/media/files/Downloads/SEN14729.pdf)). The same body comes in 2–50 kg, so one design takes the **1 kg** (delicacy), **5 kg** (default) and **20 kg** (strong grippers) cells. Retailers list the SparkFun 5 kg TAL220B as retired, so check that the cell you buy has this drawing (`cell_l`, `cell_hole_pitch`).

- **Load path.** Jaw A screws to the cell's fixed end (the cable end, −X) from below, and jaw B to its load end from above. Each jaw touches the cell only on its own 14 mm end block. A 1.5 mm pocket keeps it clear of the beam and of the other end, so the whole squeeze passes through the cell's bending beam. The pocket stops 3.5 mm past the cell end, so it is closed on all sides and prints as a 15 mm bridge. The verifier checks this with a 0.05 mm probe.
- **Dowels.** Two Ø4 × 28 steel dowels are pressed through jaw A and slide in blind holes in jaw B. They carry shear and moment; the cell sees the squeeze (the NIST split-artifact principle, [Falco et al. RA-L 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7670580)).
- **Cable.** The wires leave the −X end through the open 12.7 mm gap between the jaws; the dowels sit 6 mm beyond the cell end to leave room.
- **Swapping shapes.** The cell moves between the W30 and D30 pairs with its two M5 screws. Recalibrate after every swap.
- **Not possible in this format.** A 20 mm size would need the jaw faces closer together than the 12.7 mm cell allows. Use the FSR sandwich (PROTOCOL §4.6) for 10–20 mm.

## Build plan

Sorting sheet (print at 100 %): `docs/GRIP-9015_assy-grasp-bench-stage1-2_sort-sheet_A4.pdf`. Ready-to-slice plates: `exports/plates/`, 2 PETG plates on the X2D (about 171 g / 3.9 h and 45 g / 1.1 h, Bambu Studio estimate). Both slice without warnings.

**Stage 1: force chain**

1. **Hub box.** Melt 4 M3 inserts into the corner bosses. Slide the boards into their rail pairs, components up:
   - 4 × HX711 along the −Y wall, one per load-cell cable hole;
   - the ESP32 DevKitC at the back left, USB toward the −X port; it sits 10 mm up so the header pins hang free;
   - the INA219 at the back right, next to the two supply holes in +X.
   - Set each HX711 to 80 SPS (RATE pin high), then measure the real rate on arrival.

   ![](docs/build/step1_hub-box.png)
2. **Lid.** 4 × M3×8 into the inserts.

   ![](docs/build/step2_hub-lid.png)

**Stage 2: split artifact W30**

3. **Cell on jaw A.** Lay the cell on jaw A's raised end face, cable end (−X) over the end without the "A30" label, so the cable runs out at −X. Fit one M5×12 button head from below through the counterbore into the cell's fixed-end hole.

   ![](docs/build/step3_cell-on-jaw-a.png)
4. **Dowels.** Press 2 × Ø4 × 28 through jaw A from the outside until flush with the outer face (vice, not hammer).

   ![](docs/build/step4_dowels.png)
5. **Jaw B.** Slide jaw B down the dowels onto the cell's load end, then fit the second M5×12 from above. Check by hand that B moves only along the dowels and doesn't bind.

   ![](docs/build/step5_jaw-b.png)
6. **Calibrate.** Stand the artifact on jaw A, put the platform on jaw B (its rails straddle the jaw) and tare. Load dead weights at 5 or more points from 0 to 100 % of the cell range, loading and then unloading. Fit a line and report the residuals and the hysteresis in %FS. Repeat with the masses centred on each of the 3 marks; accept a position effect below 2 %. Weigh the platform itself and count it in the load. Before every session, check one known weight. Do one 30 min hold at 80 %FS when commissioning.

   ![](docs/build/step6_calibrate-w30.png)
7. **D30.** Same steps with the cylinder jaws; calibrate with jaw A in the V cradle.

   ![](docs/build/step7_d30-in-cradle.png)
8. **DR-1a.** Bolt it to a 2020 extrusion (2 × M5×10 + T-nuts in one slot) or to a table, melt the M3 lock insert into the +Y rail, slide a GRIP-0002 adapter in from −X up to the stop and tighten the thumb screw.

   ![](docs/build/step8_dovetail-receptacle.png)

## Checks

`python3 grasp_validation/tools/verify_grasp.py` (17/17 PASS, 2026-11-15):

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Jaw volumes vs analytic model (boxes, cylinder segments, pockets, holes) | W30 −8.3 / −9.2 mm³ (labels only); D30 +12.3 / +10.7 mm³ (counterbore on a curved face removes less than the flat-face estimate) |
| Artifact size across the squeeze faces / length | 30.000 / 86.000 (both shapes) |
| Cell vs jaws | no overlap |
| Jaws clear of the beam and of the other end block (0.05 mm probe) | no overlap |
| Dowels (drawn Ø3.8) vs press holes, slip holes, cell | no overlap; D30 dowel-in-press-hole pairs are reported **unchecked** because the pin and hole walls coincide (allowed by the verifier, every other unchecked pair fails it) |
| Platform on W30 jaw B / V cradle under D30 jaw A | resting contact (0.00), no overlap |
| Real GRIP-0002 adapter (STEP) in the DR-1a channel | resting on the floor (0.00), no overlap |
| 6 PCB envelopes (width × 1.6) in their grooves | no overlap; 0.10 mm gap measured mid-rail. Where a board crosses a rail end, the clearance check reports 0.00 without an overlap; not explained, so slide a board in to check |

Per-feature volume drops on GRIP-0098 (first build, open pocket) matched the geometry feature by feature: M5 hole 205.48, counterbore 201.04, dowel hole 98.1, label 8.34 mm³. The closed-pocket rebuild matches the analytic total within the label volume.

Not checked:
- the real HX711, ESP32 and INA219 board widths (`BOARDS` in the generator; HX711 modules come in several sizes);
- connector heights;
- the cable bend radius at the −X end;
- the gripper's own jaw opening on the 30 mm artifacts.

**NEEDS PRINT TEST:**
- dowel press fit Ø3.8 and slip fit Ø4.3 (steel in PETG);
- M3 insert holes Ø4.0;
- board grooves (0.2 mm per side, 1.85 mm slot for 1.6 mm PCBs);
- that the 15 mm pocket bridges print cleanly (inner face down).

## Regenerate

```
python3 grasp_validation/tools/gen_grasp.py
tools/build.sh grasp_validation
python3 grasp_validation/tools/verify_grasp.py
python3 grasp_validation/tools/build_guide.py
```

Key parameters (`tools/gen_grasp.py`):
- `SIZE`: artifact size; a new size gets new part numbers;
- `CELL_*`, `HOLE_X`: the cell drawing;
- `POCKET`: beam relief;
- `DOWEL_PRESS`, `DOWEL_SLIP`: dowel fits;
- `DT`: dovetail; keep it equal to GRIP-0002 / GRIP-0069;
- `BOARDS`: board widths and positions in the hub.
