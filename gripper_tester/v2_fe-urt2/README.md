# gripper_tester v2: FE-URT-2 controller — GRIP-0069, GRIP-0002, GRIP-9013

The bench baseplate for testing SO-101 style grippers, now with a mount for the **Feetech FE-URT-2** USB / TTL / RS485 bus-servo board as the controller for the gripper under test. Everything else is the same as v1 (`gripper_tester/`): grippers swap tool-free on the dovetail, and the adapter carries the STS3215 horn interface, so a gripper that fits the tester also bolts onto the SO-101 wrist.

![Fit check: baseplate, adapter, SO-101 gripper, FE-URT-2 envelope](docs/GRIP-9013_fit-check.png)

## Interfaces

- **Gripper ↔ adapter** (unchanged): STS3215 horn copy, Ø19.6 × 0.9 locating boss, 4 × M3 on Ø14 at 45°. Any SO-101 part with the horn interface fits (follower gripper, leader `Wrist_Roll`), and the same gripper mounts on the arm.
- **Adapter ↔ baseplate** (unchanged): dovetail 34 → 28 × 6, 40 long, 0.2 mm per side, slides in from −X to the end stop, M3 thumb-screw wedge lock on +Y.
- **FE-URT-2 board.** Board from shop listings: 55.8 × 36.6 × 11.5 mm (one listing says 39 wide), holes 48.8 × 28.8 apart, about Ø3. **NEEDS CHECK on the real board**: `brd_dx`, `brd_dy` are parameters.
  - Insert holes measured on the model (`model.faces`): (19.6, ±14.4) and (68.4, ±14.4), boss top z 13, hole bottom z 6 (blind, plate top z 5).
  - Board footprint x 16.1 … 71.9, y ±19.5 (39 mm worst case). USB-C and power face +X, at the plate edge; the servo bus cable runs to the gripper through the free space (gripper ends at x −10, board starts at x 16.1).
  - 8 mm under the PCB for through-hole pins and wires.

## Build

1. Melt 4 M3 inserts into the board bosses and 1 into the lock hole on the +Y rail (and 4 into the adapter, as v1).
2. Screw the FE-URT-2 onto the bosses with 4 × M3 × 6, USB-C toward the +X plate edge.
3. Bolt the gripper onto the adapter (4 × M3 × 8), slide the adapter into the dovetail from −X up to the stop, tighten the thumb screw.
4. Plug the gripper servo into the FE-URT-2 bus connector, and power + USB-C into the board.

Sorting sheet (print at 100 %): [`docs/v2_fe-urt2_sort-sheet_A4.pdf`](docs/v2_fe-urt2_sort-sheet_A4.pdf). Ready-to-slice plate: `exports/plates/` (one PETG plate, opens in Bambu Studio).
