# Strandbeest gripper v5: parallel jaws, fully printed (GRIP-9011)

X1, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.** This is [v4b](../v4b_snap-pins/README.md) (hand-operated, snap pins, nothing to buy) with **jaws that stay parallel**. In v4b each jaw hung on a single pin at the foot, so it was loose and could tip. In v5 each finger has a second, identical leg, and a 91 mm jaw is pinned to the feet of both legs (the [v3b](../v3b_twin-legs/README.md) parallelogram). Both legs move the same way, so the jaw slides along without turning. Every part except the pins carries a debossed label. Overview of all versions: [../README.md](../README.md).

![done](docs/build/step8_done_top.png)

## What changes from v4b

- **Leg B:** each finger gets a second leg, 50 mm further along the stroke, made of the same link parts as leg A.
  - A z60 idler (GRIP-0030) gears crank B (GRIP-0029 finger 1, GRIP-0032 finger 2) to crank A, so both legs run at the same crank angle.
- **Jaw (GRIP-0031, 91 mm):** pinned at foot A, it rides in a 2 mm slot at foot B, which keeps print tolerances from binding the parallelogram. It translates without turning.
  - In CAD it holds its angle to 0.003° over a full turn, and its faces stay parallel to 0.004 mm over 80 mm.
- **Frame GRIP-0060:** new. It carries both legs of each finger, the four crank axles, four idler axles and the thumb wheel.
  - The thumb wheel moved to 15° from crank 1, because at v4's 40° it would hit the z60 idler.
- **Fully printed:** 73 prints, nothing to buy. The pins are v4b's snap pins, plus one new length for the z60 idler axle (GRIP-0061).
- **J3 pins go in from the top**: the head sits on b-d-e and the barb under f. A J3 head under f (as in v4b) would hit the z60 idler, which is also 1.2 mm thinner (4.3 mm) for the same reason.
- **Labels:** every link, gear, jaw and the frame has a debossed name (0.6 mm deep). The labelled parts are revision **X2**: same fit as X1, plus the label. v4b keeps X1.

| Part | Label | Where |
|---|---|---|
| j, k, c, f links (GRIP-0015…0018) | **J**, **K**, **C**, **F** | top face, middle of the bar |
| b-d-e triangle (GRIP-0019 / 0021) | **UP1** / **UP2** | top face (already in v4b) |
| g-h-i triangle (GRIP-0023 / 0024) | **UP1** / **UP2** | top face |
| crank gears | **A1** (0034), **A2** (0013), **B1** (0029), **B2** (0032) | top face, opposite the J1 hub |
| idlers | **Z33** (0014), **Z60** (0030) | top face |
| jaw (GRIP-0031) | **JAW** | top face, between the two pins |
| frame (GRIP-0060) | **V5** | plate top |
| snap pins, J1 pin, thumb wheel | none | the pins are too small: tell them apart by length (table below). The wheel is unique. |

## Build plan

About 1.5 hours. **Tools:** flat-nose pliers to take a pin out. **Parts:** the 73 prints above.

In the pictures, the parts added in each step are **orange** and the parts already in place are grey (rendered from the CAD assembly by `../tools/build_guide_v5.py`).

**How every snap pin goes in.** Push it through all the parts of its joint, head first, until the barb clicks over the far side. Then turn the joint: it must move freely. To take a pin out, pinch the prongs with pliers and push it back.
- **J3** goes in **from the top**, with the head on b-d-e.
- **Every other pin** goes in **from below**.

### Before you start

1. Sort the links into **four legs**:
   - finger 1: leg A and leg B, from the UP1 triangles;
   - finger 2: leg A and leg B, from the UP2 triangles.
   Every leg has one each of J, K, C, F, b-d-e and g-h-i.
2. Sort the gears by their labels: A1, A2, B1, B2, two Z33 and two Z60.

### Step 1: gear train on the frame

![step 1](docs/build/step1_gear-train.png)

1. Put the frame (GRIP-0060) plate down. Place the gears on their axle holes:
   - **A1** next to the thumb wheel, **A2** at the far end, with the two **Z33** idlers between them;
   - **B1** and **B2** on the crank holes 50 mm from A1 and A2, on the side away from the thumb wheel, each with a **Z60** idler between it and its A gear;
   - the **thumb wheel** next to A1.
2. **Timing:** turn all four crank gears so that their J1 holes point as in the top view below. Then let the idlers drop into mesh. Legs A and B of a finger must sit at the same crank angle.
3. Lift the frame a little and push the axle pins up from under the base:
   - **GRIP-0048** through each crank gear (the barb snaps into the recess on top);
   - **GRIP-0061** through each Z60 idler;
   - **GRIP-0049** through each Z33 idler and through the wheel.
4. Turn the wheel: all nine gears must turn together without binding.

![step 1, top view: crank timing](docs/build/step1_gear-train_top.png)

### Steps 2–5: build each leg in your hand (4 legs)

Build one leg at a time, the same way for all four:
- legs A and B of finger 1 from the UP1 triangles;
- legs A and B of finger 2 from the UP2 triangles.

Stack the links from the bottom up, labels facing up, and pin each joint once all its parts are on.

**Step 2: F and b-d-e, pin J3 (from the top).** Lay the F link (hub up), then put the b-d-e triangle (UP mark up) on it. Push **GRIP-0045** **down** through b-d-e and F at J3, so that the head sits on b-d-e and the barb clicks under F.

![step 2](docs/build/step2_leg.png)

**Step 3: C and g-h-i, pin J5.**
1. Lay the C link over b-d-e at G. It is held later, by J4 and G.
2. Put the g-h-i triangle (UP mark up) on the F hub and on C at J4.
3. Push **GRIP-0047** up through F and g-h-i at J5.

![step 3](docs/build/step3_leg.png)

**Step 4: J, pin J2.** Put the J link on the b-d-e hub. Push **GRIP-0047** up from under b-d-e through J at J2.

![step 4](docs/build/step4_leg.png)

**Step 5: K, pin J4.** Put the K link on top at J4. Push **GRIP-0046** up from under C, through C, g-h-i and K. The leg is now one piece that folds freely. The J1 ends of J and K, the G holes of b-d-e and C, and the foot hole F stay open.

![step 5](docs/build/step5_leg.png)

### Step 6: mount the four legs

![step 6](docs/build/step6_mount.png)

1. Put each leg on the frame:
   - the **G holes** go over the G post;
   - the **J1 holes** of J and K go over the J1 hole of their crank gear (A legs on A1/A2, B legs on B1/B2).
2. Push **GRIP-0051** up from under the base through the post, b-d-e and C at G.
3. Press a **J1 shoulder pin GRIP-0043** in from the top, through K and J, into the crank gear until the head sits on K. Support the gear from below while you press.
4. Turn the wheel once slowly. Legs A and B of each finger must move identically: their feet stay 50 mm apart.

### Step 7: jaws

![step 7](docs/build/step7_jaws.png)

1. Turn the wheel to the clamp position: the feet closest together, as in the step 8 picture.
2. Lay a jaw (label up) over the two feet of a finger. Its round hole goes on foot A (the leg nearer the thumb wheel), its slot on foot B.
3. Push **GRIP-0050** up from under the g-h-i plate through foot A and the jaw, then the second one through foot B and the jaw slot.
4. Repeat for the other finger.

### Step 8: check

![step 8](docs/build/step8_done_top.png)

1. Turn the thumb wheel slowly through a full crank turn: every joint must move without binding.
2. **The jaws must stay parallel** to each other the whole time, and meet with a gap of about 1.5 mm along their full length.
3. **If a jaw binds or twists:** legs A and B of that finger are out of time. Pull the Z60 idler pin, lift the Z60 idler, turn crank B one tooth and put it back.
4. **If one jaw arrives early:** finger 1 and finger 2 are out of time. Shift one Z33 idler by one tooth (see [v4b](../v4b_snap-pins/README.md#step-8-check)).

## Link lengths vs jaw opening and feed stroke

Derived with `python3 ../tools/jaw_relations.py`. It uses the same Jansen kinematics as the generator, with all links × S (here S = 0.4), crank-to-crank distance D (here 86 mm) and jaw thickness w (11 mm). y_min is the lowest foot point and H the step height of the foot path.

| Quantity | Formula | v5 (S = 0.4, D = 86, w = 11) | Verified in CAD |
|---|---|---|---|
| clamp gap | D − 2·\|y_min\| − w = D − 183.7·S − w | 1.52 mm | 1.53 mm |
| jaw opening above clamp | 2·H = 44.9·S | 17.97 mm | 19.50 − 1.53 = 17.97 mm |
| max gap | D − 138.8·S − w | 19.5 mm | 19.50 mm |
| feed stroke while clamped (gap within 1.0·S of clamp) | ≈ 50.3·S | 20.1 mm | 19.9 mm |
| full foot stride | 67.9·S | 27.2 mm | |

**What it means:**
- **The legs set the opening and the stroke.** Both grow in proportion to S, so a bigger gripper is a uniform scale-up.
- **The gear distance D only moves the clamp gap.** It shifts the closed and open gap together and leaves the opening and stroke alone.
- **Changing a single link is sensitive, and the leg runs near its limits.** For +1 % on one link (others fixed):
  - **k:** opening +39 %, stroke −7 %;
  - **c:** opening −17 %;
  - **a (frame):** opening −15 %;
  - **j:** stroke −9 %, opening +7 %;
  - **e, h and f:** stroke −14 %, −16 % and −10 %.
  - The crank m alone does little at +1 % (opening +7 %, stroke +0.9 %), but it is the practical knob downwards: m −10 % gives an opening of 11.0 mm and a stroke of 18.2 mm.
- **Limits:** the leg only closes over a full turn for m up to **+7 %**, k up to **+1.8 %** and c down to **−2.9 %**. Change the holy-number ratios only with this script, never by hand.

## Fit (NEEDS PRINT TEST)

As in [v4b](../v4b_snap-pins/README.md#fit-needs-print-test): 0.15 mm play per side on the pins, a Ø4.0 barb over the Ø3.4 holes, and a J1 press fit (Ø2.7 tip in Ø2.5). New in v5:
- **Jaw slot at foot B:** 2 mm long, so the parallelogram doesn't bind if the legs are slightly out. If the jaw still binds, file the slot longer.
- **Z60 idler:** 4.3 mm thick (was 5.5). It is less face width for leg B's drive, but enough for hand force.
