# Strandbeest gripper v7: sandwich frame (GRIP-9014)

X1, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.** This is [v6](../v6_mirror-legs/README.md) with **every fixed axis held at both ends**: v6's axes stood up from the base plate only and bent too much to use. v7 puts the mechanism between the base frame and two top plates on pillars. The kinematics are v6's: mirrored twin legs, parallel 135 mm jaws, same clamp gap and stroke. Overview of all versions: [../README.md](../README.md).

![done](docs/build/step10_done_iso.png)

## Why v6 bent, and what v7 changes

In v6, three things were held from below only:
- the **crank gears** turned on a Ø3 pin in a 14 mm boss and carried an **18 mm tall J1 hub**, with the J and K links at its top;
- the **G pivots** sat on **24 mm tall Ø7 posts**;
- the J1 shoulder pin ended in free air on top of K.

A straight axle from the base to a top plate doesn't work for v6's layout. The K link sweeps right over the G pivot, and J and K sweep over the crank centre O (that is how a Jansen leg moves). So no column can run through there. v7 solves it like this:

| Axis | v6 | v7 |
|---|---|---|
| Crank (×4) | gear on a base pin, J1 hub 18 mm up to J/K | gear on a base pin **+ crank arm on top** (GRIP-0072) turning on a pin in the top plate. The **J1 pin is pressed into both**, so J1 and the J and K links on it are held top and bottom. |
| G (×4) | one 24 mm post from the base, b-d-e and C on it | **two short pivots on one axis:** b-d-e on a 9 mm post from the base, **C on a stub hanging from the top plate**. K runs between them. |
| Thumb wheel | rim under the gears, so every gear sat on 14 mm bosses | gear in the gear layer, its **hub turns in the top plate**, and the **knob is on top** (GRIP-0073, hex drive). The gear layer drops onto the base: every boss is 0.5 mm, every post is shorter. |
| Link layers | 7 link layers, stack top z 49.2, jaws to z 62.2 | **6 link layers + the arm layer**, top plate z 38–42, jaws z 34.5–42.5, knob top z 50.5 |
| Idlers Z33 / Z20 | base pin, gear 5.5 / 4.3 mm | same parts on a base pin, now 0.5 mm above the plate. **Not sandwiched**: the g-h-i plates sweep over their axes. They carry only gear-tooth load on a 6–8 mm pin. |

**Link layer order** (z bottom of each layer): b-d-e 13.5, f and j 17.0, (empty) 20.5, k 24.0, g-h-i 27.5, c 31.0, crank arm 34.5. It was searched like `../tools/layers.py`, with two extra rules: the J1 column runs from the gear to the arm, and K must lie between b-d-e and C with a free layer on each side (snap-pin barbs). Because every link's hubs change, all links have new part numbers.

**Top plates.** There are two identical plates (GRIP-0071, 147 × 70 × 4 mm plus the G stubs). Finger 2's plate is finger 1's turned 180°. That is also why each plate has two Ø10.4 hub holes: only finger 1's wheel-side hole carries the wheel hub, and the other is unused. Each plate sits on **4 pillars** (Ø12, part of the frame) that stand outside everything that moves. The plates stop 7 mm short of the jaw path, so the **gripping slot stays open** from above. The base also has a notch under the free 40 mm of the jaws.

| | v6 | v7 |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | 138 × 69 × 28 mm | **161 × 168 × 38 mm**, the pillars set the size |
| Height (base to top) | ~64 mm (jaw pin barbs) | **52 mm** (wheel pin barb on the knob) |
| Prints | 73 | **96** (top plates, arms, knob and pillar pins added) |
| Clamp gap, max gap, feed stroke | 1.53 / 19.5 / 19.9 mm | same |

## Build plan

About 2 hours. **Tools:** flat-nose pliers (to take a pin out), a small clamp or vice for the press fits. In the pictures, parts added in a step are **orange** and parts already in place are grey (`../tools/build_guide_v7.py`).

**Sorting sheet:** print `docs/GRIP-9014_assy-sandwich_sort-sheet_A4.pdf` at **100 %** (the 50 mm bar checks the scale). Lay each part on its outline.

**Pins by length** (shank = head to barb shoulder; all D3 snap pins with two flats):

| Pin | Shank | Qty | Goes in |
|---|---|---|---|
| GRIP-0088 | 6.0 | 4 | crank gear axles, from below |
| GRIP-0089 | 6.5 | 2 | Z20 idler axles, from below |
| GRIP-0090 | 6.7 | 8 | J2 and J3, **from the top** (head on j / f, barb under b-d-e) |
| GRIP-0091 | 7.7 | 6 | Z33 idler axles (from below) and crank arms (**from the top** of the top plate) |
| GRIP-0092 | 10.2 | 4 | J4, from below (head under k, barb on c) |
| GRIP-0093 | 11.2 | 4 | upper G pivot, **from the top** (through the plate stub into C) |
| GRIP-0094 | 13.7 | 8 | J5 (from below, head under f) and lower G pivot (from under the base into b-d-e) |
| GRIP-0095 | 15.2 | 4 | jaw pins, from below (head under g-h-i) |
| GRIP-0096 | 39.2 | 8 | pillars, from under the base up through the top plate |
| GRIP-0097 | 47.7 | 1 | thumb-wheel axle, from under the base up through the knob |

### Step 1: gear train on the frame

![step 1](docs/build/step1_gear-train.png)

1. Put the frame (GRIP-0070) plate down. Place the crank gears **A1, A2, B1, B2**, the two **Z33** and two **Z20** idlers, and the **thumb-wheel gear** (GRIP-0074, hub up) on their bosses.
2. **Timing:** turn the crank gears so that their J1 holes point as in the top view, then drop the idlers into mesh (as v6).
3. Push the axle pins up from under the base: GRIP-0088 into the crank gears, GRIP-0091 into the Z33s, GRIP-0089 into the Z20s. The wheel gear gets its pin in step 8.
4. Turn the wheel gear by its hub: all gears turn, and A and B of each finger turn the opposite way.

![step 1 top](docs/build/step1_gear-train_top.png)

### Steps 2–4: build each leg in your hand (4 legs)

Stack from the bottom up, labels up.

**Step 2: b-d-e and f, pin J3.** Lay b-d-e (mark up), put F on it at J3 (hub up). Push **GRIP-0090** down from the top through F and b-d-e: the head sits on F and the barb clicks under b-d-e.

![step 2](docs/build/step2_leg.png)

**Step 3: J, pin J2.** Put J (hub up) on b-d-e at J2. Push **GRIP-0090** down through J and b-d-e.

![step 3](docs/build/step3_leg.png)

**Step 4: K, g-h-i, C, pins J4 and J5.** Put K over the J hub at J1. Stack g-h-i (mark up) on the F hub at J5 and over K at J4, then C on top at J4. Push **GRIP-0092** up from under K through K, g-h-i and C. Push **GRIP-0094** up from under F through F and g-h-i at J5. The G holes of b-d-e and C and the J1 holes of J and K stay open.

![step 4](docs/build/step4_leg.png)

*Leg B, step 2 (finger 1, UP2 triangle, mark upside down):*

![leg B](docs/build/legB_step2.png)

### Step 5: mount the legs

![step 5](docs/build/step5_mount.png)

1. **Press a J1 pin (GRIP-0075)** into each crank gear: the short tip goes into the gear's Ø2.5 hole. Support the gear from below.
2. Put each leg on the frame:
   - J1 holes of J and K over the J1 pin;
   - b-d-e's G hole on the base post;
   - legs A over cranks A1/A2, legs B over B1/B2, the legs of a finger back to back.
3. Push **GRIP-0094** up from under the base through the post into b-d-e (lower G pivot).

### Step 6: crank arms

![step 6](docs/build/step6_arms.png)

Press a **crank arm (GRIP-0072)** onto the top tip of each J1 pin, hub down: the J1 tip goes into the arm's Ø2.5 hole. Turn the arm so that its O hole lies right above the crank gear centre.

### Step 7: top plates

![step 7](docs/build/step7_top-plates.png)

1. Lower each top plate (GRIP-0071, label up) onto its four pillars:
   - the **G stubs** go into the C links' G holes;
   - finger 1's plate slides over the **wheel hub**.
2. Push **GRIP-0096** up from under the base through each pillar and the plate (8×).
3. Push **GRIP-0091** down from the top through the plate into each crank arm's O hole (4×). Turn the wheel a little if an arm doesn't line up.
4. Push **GRIP-0093** down from the top through each G stub into C (4×).

### Step 8: knob

![step 8](docs/build/step8_knob.png)

Put the knob (GRIP-0073) on the hex key of the wheel hub and push **GRIP-0097** up from under the base through the wheel gear, hub and knob until the barb clicks on top.

### Step 9: jaws

![step 9](docs/build/step9_jaws.png)

As in v6: the jaw's round hole goes on foot A and the slot over foot B. Push **GRIP-0095** up from under each g-h-i plate through the foot and the jaw (4×).

### Step 10: check

![step 10](docs/build/step10_done_top.png)

1. Turn the knob slowly through a full turn (= 2 crank turns): nothing binds, and the foot-B pin slides along the whole slot.
2. Press the jaws down and sideways: **the legs must not tilt** any more (that was v6's problem).
3. The jaws stay parallel and close to about 1.5 mm. If a jaw tilts, legs A and B of that finger are out of time: take the top plate off, lift the Z20 idler and turn crank B by one tooth (as in v6).

## Fit (NEEDS PRINT TEST)

As v6: 0.15 mm play per side on the pins, Ø4.0 barbs over the Ø3.4 holes. New in v7:
- **J1 pin pressed in at both ends** (Ø2.7 tips in Ø2.5 holes, gear and arm). If the arm's press fit is loose, the arm can slip on the pin; a drop of glue in the arm is fine, but then the leg no longer comes apart.
- **Wheel hub Ø10 in a Ø10.4 hole** of the top plate (0.2 mm per side) and **hex key AF 7.0 in a 7.3 socket**.
- **Top plates on the pillars:** if the plate rocks, the pillar tops are uneven: sand them flat.
- **Crank arm vs gear alignment:** the arm turns on the top-plate pin at O while the gear turns on the base pin at O. Both are positioned by the printed frame and plate holes. If the crank binds, ream the arm's O hole to Ø3.5.
