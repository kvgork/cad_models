# Strandbeest gripper v4a: v4 with printed pins (GRIP-9008)

X1, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.** This is [v4](../v4_thumb-wheel/README.md) (hand-operated, thumb wheel) with **every fastener printed**: no screws, nuts or washers to buy. It is meant as a functional test of the mechanism, not a durable gripper. Overview of all versions: [../README.md](../README.md).

![pins](docs/GRIP-9008_assy-printed-pins_pins.png)

*In the picture the v4 parts are see-through, the printed pins are orange and the caps are blue.*

## What changes from v4

- **The v4 parts are reused unchanged** (GRIP-0013…0035): the same STL files, with the same Ø3.4 holes.
- **Pins (GRIP-0036…0042):** a Ø3.0 shank with a Ø5.5 × 3 head, in the existing Ø3.4 holes (0.2 mm play per side). There is one part number per shank length. A cap pressed onto the shank holds the pin in.
- **Caps (GRIP-0044):** a Ø6 × 2 disc with a Ø2.8 hole, pressed onto the pin (0.2 mm interference). On the crank axles the cap sits in the gear-top recess where v4 had the thin nut.
- **J1 (GRIP-0043):** a printed shoulder pin replaces the ISO 7379 screw. It has a Ø4 × 24.5 shoulder in the Ø4.2 bores, a Ø7 head on top and a Ø2.7 tip that presses into the Ø2.5 hole in the crank gear.
- **No washers:** the links run directly against the pin heads and caps, with 0.5 mm of axial play per layer.
- **Lengths:** each pin is the stack height + 2 mm for the cap, rounded up to 0.5 mm, so a pin tip sticks out of its cap by at most 0.3 mm. Heads and caps stay inside the space that v4's screw heads and nyloc nuts were laid out for (`../tools/layers.py`), so nothing new collides.

## Printing the pins

- **Plain pins (GRIP-0036…0042):** print them lying on their side, on the 0.25 mm flat that runs along the shank and head. Use "lay on face" in the slicer. Lying down, the layers run along the pin, so it doesn't snap at a layer line.
- **J1 shoulder pin (GRIP-0043):** print it standing on its head, tip up. It has no flat, because the shoulder is a bearing surface. Use 100 % infill, and a brim if it wobbles.
- **Caps (GRIP-0044):** print flat.
- **Settings:** PETG, 0.2 mm layers, at least 3 perimeters, so that the pins are almost solid.
- **Spares:** print 4–6 extra caps and a couple of each pin, because press fits are hit and miss on a first print.

## Fit (NEEDS PRINT TEST)

| Fit | CAD | If it is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Pin in link hole | Ø3.0 in Ø3.4 | Too loose: raise `ppin_d` to 3.1–3.2. Too tight: sand the pin. |
| Cap on pin | Ø2.8 hole on Ø3.0 pin | Falls off: lower `cap_bore` to 2.7, or add a drop of CA glue. Splits: raise it to 2.9. |
| J1 tip in crank gear | Ø2.7 tip in Ø2.5 hole | Too loose: glue it. Too tight: lower `j1_tip_d`. |

The parameters are in the assembly script (`cad/GRIP-9008_assy-printed-pins.json`). Change them in `../tools/gen_v3.py` (class `PrintedPins`), then rebuild.

## Assembly

The assembly order and gear timing are the same as for [v4](../v4_thumb-wheel/README.md#hardware-notes):
1. Push each pin through from the head side, the same way the v4 screw went: axles and G from below the base, J2…J5 from under the lowest link, and jaw pins from under the g-h-i plate.
2. Press its cap on until it seats, then check that the joint still turns freely.
3. Press the J1 pins in from the top, through k and j, into the crank gear.
