## What it is

Each finger gets a second, identical leg B shifted 50 mm along the stroke. Leg B's crank (GRIP-0029 finger 1, GRIP-0032 finger 2) is geared to crank A through a Ø60 idler (GRIP-0030, qty 2, 60 teeth), so both cranks turn at the same angle.

- **Jaw:** the 91 mm jaw (GRIP-0031, qty 2) is pinned at foot A, rides in a 2 mm slot at foot B, and overhangs leg B by 30 mm. Because both legs move identically, it translates without turning, keeping the faces exactly parallel along their whole length. The slot stops print tolerances from binding the parallelogram.
- **Frame:** GRIP-0028, bigger, carrying the B posts and both idlers.
- **Legs B:** the same parts as legs A: GRIP-0015…0019/0021 plus GRIP-0023/0024. The print kit in `exports/` lists each part once; print every link twice.
- **Extra hardware:** v2's leg hardware a second time, plus:
  - 2 × M3×14 + nyloc (idler axles);
  - 2 × M3×12 + thin nut (crank B axles);
  - 4 × M3×30 + nyloc (jaw pins).
