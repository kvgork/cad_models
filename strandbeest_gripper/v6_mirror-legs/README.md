# Strandbeest gripper v6: mirrored twin legs, long parallel jaws (GRIP-9012)

X1, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.** This is [v5](../v5_parallel-jaws/README.md) made compact: same parallel jaws, same fully printed snap-pin build, same labels, but **leg B is leg A mirrored**. ## How the mirrored leg keeps the jaw parallel

- **Leg B is leg A reflected** about a line across the stroke, 12 mm from crank A. The cranks are 24 mm apart and the legs stay at least 5 mm clear over a full turn.
- **Foot B moves up and down exactly like foot A**, at every crank angle, but the opposite way along the stroke.
- **The jaw is pinned on foot A and slides on foot B in a long slot along the jaw.** Both feet are always at the same height, so the line between them never tilts and the jaw cannot turn: it stays parallel to the other jaw.
- **The slot is long because** foot B travels 26.9 … 81.2 mm from foot A along the jaw: twice the foot's stride.
- **Crank B turns the other way to crank A.** It is driven from the Z33 idler through a small **Z20 idler** (crank A → Z33 → Z20 → crank B).
  - The cranks can't mesh with each other directly: at 20 mm apart each J1 hub would sweep over the other gear's teeth, and a bigger crank gear would hit the G post.
  - The Z20 idler is 4.3 mm thick, so the J3 pin barbs clear it, as the Z60 did in v5.
- **Leg B's triangles are the *other* finger's triangles, turned 180°.** A mirrored UP1 triangle is exactly the UP2 part. So finger 1's leg B uses UP2 triangles and finger 2's leg B uses UP1 triangles; their UP mark then reads upside down. The links (J, K, C, F) are the same parts in every leg.
- **Thumb wheel:** on the outside of crank A1, away from both fingers.

## Labels

As in [v5](../v5_parallel-jaws/README.md#what-changes-from-v4b): J/K/C/F on the links, UP1/UP2 on the triangles, A1/A2/B1/B2 on the crank gears, and V6 on the frame. Also new in v6:
- **Z33** and **Z20** on the idlers;
- **JAW** on the jaws.

The B crank gears are new parts:
- **B1** (GRIP-0063) and **B2** (GRIP-0064): their teeth sit differently relative to the J1 hole. Don't swap them with A1/A2.

## Build plan

About 1.5 hours. **Tools:** flat-nose pliers to take a pin out. **Parts:** the 73 prints above. In the pictures, the parts added in each step are **orange** and the parts already in place are grey (`../tools/build_guide_v6.py`).

**Snap pins:**
- **J3** goes in **from the top**: head on b-d-e, barb under F.
- **Every other pin** goes in **from below**.
- Push until the barb clicks, then check that the joint turns.
- To take a pin out, pinch the prongs.

### Before you start

1. Sort the links into **four legs**. Every leg has one each of J, K, C, F, b-d-e and g-h-i.

   | Leg | Triangles | Mark reads |
   |---|---|---|
   | finger 1, leg A | **UP1** | upright |
   | finger 1, leg B | **UP2** | upside down when mounted |
   | finger 2, leg A | **UP2** | upright |
   | finger 2, leg B | **UP1** | upside down when mounted |

   So you print 2 × UP1 and 2 × UP2 of each triangle, as in the print list.

### Step 1: gear train on the frame

![step 1](docs/build/step1_gear-train.png)

1. Put the frame (GRIP-0062) plate down. Place:
   - **A1** next to the thumb-wheel hole, **A2** at the opposite end, and the two **Z33** idlers in a line between them;
   - **B1** and **B2** 24 mm beside A1 and A2, and a **Z20** idler between each Z33 and its B gear;
   - the **thumb wheel** on the outside of A1.
2. **Timing:** turn all four crank gears so that their J1 holes point as in the top view below, then drop the idlers into mesh. Crank B's J1 is the mirror image of crank A's.
3. Push the axle pins up from under the base:
   - **GRIP-0048** through each crank gear;
   - **GRIP-0061** through each Z20;
   - **GRIP-0049** through each Z33 and through the wheel.
4. Turn the wheel: all nine gears turn without binding, and A and B of each finger turn in **opposite** directions.

![step 1, top view: timing](docs/build/step1_gear-train_top.png)

### Steps 2–5: build each leg in your hand (4 legs)

Every leg is built the same way and in the same order, also the mirrored B legs; only the triangles differ (see the table above). Stack from the bottom up, labels up.

**Step 2: F and b-d-e, pin J3 (from the top).** Lay the F link (hub up), then the b-d-e triangle (mark up) on it. Push **GRIP-0045** down through b-d-e and F at J3: the head sits on b-d-e and the barb clicks under F.

![step 2](docs/build/step2_leg.png)

*The same step for a mirrored leg B (finger 1, UP2 triangle, mark upside down):*

![step 2, leg B](docs/build/legB_step2.png)

**Step 3: C and g-h-i, pin J5.** Lay C over b-d-e at G. Put g-h-i (mark up) on the F hub and on C at J4. Push **GRIP-0047** up through F and g-h-i at J5.

![step 3](docs/build/step3_leg.png)

**Step 4: J, pin J2.** Put J on the b-d-e hub and push **GRIP-0047** up from under b-d-e through J.

![step 4](docs/build/step4_leg.png)

**Step 5: K, pin J4.** Put K on top at J4 and push **GRIP-0046** up from under C, through C, g-h-i and K. The J1 ends of J and K, the G holes and the foot F stay open.

![step 5](docs/build/step5_leg.png)

### Step 6: mount the four legs

![step 6](docs/build/step6_mount.png)

1. Put each leg on the frame:
   - leg A over crank A1/A2, leg B over crank B1/B2, the two legs of a finger back to back;
   - the **G holes** over the G post;
   - the **J1 holes** over the crank's J1 hole.
2. Push **GRIP-0051** up from under the base through the post, b-d-e and C at G.
3. Press a **J1 shoulder pin GRIP-0043** in from the top through K and J into the crank gear. Support the gear from below.
4. Turn the wheel slowly. The two feet of each finger must always be at the **same height**, moving apart and together along the stroke.

### Step 7: jaws

![step 7](docs/build/step7_jaws.png)

1. Lay a jaw (label up) over the feet of a finger:
   - the **round hole** goes on **foot A**, on the side the jaw sticks out;
   - the **slot** goes over foot B.
2. Push **GRIP-0050** up from under the g-h-i plate through foot A and the jaw hole.
3. Push the second one through foot B and the jaw slot.
4. Repeat for the other finger.

### Step 8: check

![step 8](docs/build/step8_done_top.png)

1. Turn the thumb wheel slowly through a full crank turn: every joint moves without binding, and the foot-B pin slides freely along the whole slot.
2. **The jaws stay parallel** and meet with a gap of about 1.5 mm along their full length.
3. **If a jaw tilts:** the two legs of that finger are out of time. Pull the Z20 idler pin, lift the Z20 and turn crank B by a tooth.
4. **If one jaw arrives early:** shift a Z33 idler by one tooth, as in v4b.

## Link lengths vs jaw opening and feed stroke

Unchanged from v5: the legs are the same. Clamp gap = D − 183.7·S − w, max gap = D − 138.8·S − w, feed stroke ≈ 50·S (S = 0.4, D = 86, w = 11). See [v5](../v5_parallel-jaws/README.md#link-lengths-vs-jaw-opening-and-feed-stroke) and `../tools/jaw_relations.py`.

**New in v6:** the slot length is set by the full stride, slot ≈ 2 × 67.9·S = 54.3 mm. A bigger S makes the jaw longer too.

## Fit (NEEDS PRINT TEST)

As in v4b/v5: 0.15 mm play per side on the pins, Ø4.0 barbs over the Ø3.4 holes, and the J1 press fit. New in v6:
- **Long jaw slot (3.4 wide, 54 mm):** the foot-B pin slides in it all the time. If it drags, ream the slot to 3.5 or lightly grease it. If the jaw rattles, its tilt is held only by the slot's 0.15 mm play per side.
- **Z20 idler, 4.3 mm thick, z20:** two of them now carry leg B's drive.
