## What it is

The v2 pad grows a 5 mm nose with a Ø6 tip. Its inner edge sits on the pad's clamp line, recessed 1.7 mm so the two noses never collide over a full turn (closest 0.52 mm).

- **How it grips:** the nose turns with the foot, so the gap between the nose tips closes from about 6.0 to 1.3 mm along the stroke while the pads sit at 1.53 mm. It grips small items near the end of the stroke.
- **Why not longer:** a longer rigid nose needs more recess and stops gripping: 8 mm needs 5.3 mm of recess, 12 mm needs 11.1 mm. Use v3a or v3b for real jaws.
- **Parts added to v2:** GRIP-0026/0027 (g-h-i with pad + nose, finger 1/2) replace GRIP-0020/0022. No extra hardware.
