# Strandbeest gripper v4b: v4 with snap pins (GRIP-9009)

X1, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.** This is [v4](../v4_thumb-wheel/README.md) (hand-operated, thumb wheel), fully printed with as few parts as possible. **39 prints, nothing to buy**, against 56 for [v4a](../v4a_printed-pins/README.md). Every pin is one piece that snaps in, so there are no caps. Overview of all versions: [../README.md](../README.md).

![pins](docs/GRIP-9009_assy-snap-pins_pins.png)

*In the picture the v4 parts are see-through and the printed pins are orange.*

## What changes from v4 / v4a

- **The v4 parts are reused unchanged** (GRIP-0013…0035): the same STL files, with the same Ø3.4 holes.
- **Snap pins (GRIP-0045…0051):** a Ø5.5 × 3 head, a Ø3.1 shank (0.15 mm play per side, `pin_clear`) and a barbed tip.
  - The barb is Ø4.0 at its shoulder, tapering to Ø2.8 over 1.5 mm. It catches 0.3 mm per side on the top face of the stack, 0.2 mm above it.
  - A 0.8 mm slot splits the tip into two prongs, 5.7 mm deep. The prongs close by 0.3 mm each as the barb passes through the hole.
  - There is one part number per shank length (head top to barb shoulder).
  - Heads and barbs stay inside the space that v4's screw heads and nyloc nuts were laid out for, so nothing new collides.
- **J1:** the same printed shoulder pin as v4a (GRIP-0043): a Ø3.9 shoulder in the Ø4.2 bores and a Ø2.7 tip pressed into the crank gear.
- **No washers:** the links run directly against the pin heads and barbs.

## Print plate (Bambu Studio)

**`exports/plates/GRIP-9009_assy-snap-pins_plate1-petg_X1.3mf`** holds all 39 parts on one 256 × 256 mm plate (X1, P1, A1), each laid flat in its print orientation. Open it in Bambu Studio, choose PETG and your printer, then slice. The plate layout is in `exports/plates/…_plate1-petg_X1.png`. For an A1 mini, run `python3 tools/plates.py strandbeest_gripper/v4b_snap-pins --bed 180`.

Before slicing:
- Add a brim to the two J1 pins (GRIP-0043). They stand 33 mm tall on a Ø7 head.
- Check that the snap pins lie on their flat, with the slot standing upright.

## Printing the pins

- **Snap pins (GRIP-0045…0051):** print them lying on the 0.25 mm flat that runs along the head and shank. The plate file already has them that way. The layers then run along the pin and the prongs, so the prongs bend instead of snapping. The slot stands upright, so it doesn't need to bridge.
- **J1 shoulder pin (GRIP-0043):** print it standing on its head, with 100 % infill.
- **Settings:** PETG, 0.2 mm layers, at least 3 perimeters, so the pins are almost solid.
- **Spares:** print 2–3 extra of each pin, because the snap fit is untested.

## Fit (NEEDS PRINT TEST)

| Fit | CAD | If it is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Pin in link hole, J1 shoulder in its bore | 0.15 mm play per side (`CLEAR` in `gen_v3.py`): Ø3.1 in Ø3.4, Ø3.9 in Ø4.2 | Too loose: set `CLEAR` to 0.10. Too tight: sand the pin. |
| Snap barb | Ø4.0 over the Ø3.4 hole, with a 0.8 mm slot | Won't go in: lower `barb_d` to 3.8 or widen `snap_slot` to 1.0. Pops out: raise `barb_d` to 4.2. A prong breaks: widen `snap_slot` and print with more perimeters. |
| Barb axial play | the shoulder sits 0.2 mm above the stack top (`BARB_UP`) | Doesn't latch: lower `BARB_UP` to 0.1. |
| J1 tip in crank gear | Ø2.7 tip in Ø2.5 hole | Too loose: glue it. Too tight: lower `j1_tip_d`. |

Change the values in `../tools/gen_v3.py` (class `SnapPins`), then regenerate with `gen_v3.py s` and rebuild. The D-flat and the shank lengths are computed there.

## Assembly

The assembly order and gear timing are the same as for [v4](../v4_thumb-wheel/README.md#hardware-notes):
1. Push each snap pin in from the head side, the same way the v4 screw went, until it clicks: axles and G from below the base, J2…J5 from under the lowest link, and jaw pins from under the g-h-i plate. Then check that the joint turns freely.
2. Press the J1 pins in from the top, through k and j, into the crank gear.
3. To take a pin out, pinch the two prongs together with pliers and push it back out.
