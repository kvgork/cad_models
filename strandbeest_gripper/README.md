# strandbeest_gripper: Jansen-linkage (Strandbeest) gripper

Theo Jansen's leg ("holy numbers") × 0.4 on an STS3215 servo, used as a gripper finger. The flat bottom of the foot loop clamps and carries an item, and the lift releases it. Design background: vault `05-Wiki/research/2026-11-06-strandbeest-jansen-linkage-grippers.md`.

## Versions: one folder each

Each version folder holds its own `cad/` (generated command script + built `.sc`), `exports/` (a **complete print kit**: every part that version needs, including parts shared with other versions), `docs/` (pictures) and `README.md` (assembly, hardware, checks).

**File names.** Files are `<part number>_<name>`, for example `exports/GRIP-0015_link-j-hub_X1.stl`, and the names come from `PARTS.md`. Components in the GUI browser carry the same name plus `_F1`/`_F2` for the finger (and `B` for v3b's second leg).

**Building.** Every assembly is generated by a Python script in `tools/`, then built:

```bash
python3 strandbeest_gripper/tools/gen_dual.py            # writes v2_dual-finger/cad/GRIP-9003_assy-dual-finger.json
tools/build.sh strandbeest_gripper/v2_dual-finger         # builds the .sc and the exports
python3 strandbeest_gripper/tools/verify_dual.py          # acceptance checks
```

**Link lengths vs jaw opening and stroke.** `python3 strandbeest_gripper/tools/jaw_relations.py` derives these from the kinematics:
- clamp gap = D − 183.7·S − w;
- max gap = D − 138.8·S − w;
- opening = 44.9·S;
- feed stroke ≈ 50·S.

Here S is the link scale, D the crank-to-crank distance and w the jaw width. The script also gives the sensitivity to each link (see [v5](v5_parallel-jaws/README.md#link-lengths-vs-jaw-opening-and-feed-stroke)).

`tools/` is shared by all versions:
- `jansen.py`: kinematics; asserts the published reference.
- `layers.py`: works out the layer stack.
- `gen_*.py` / `verify_*.py`: one generator and one check script per version.

**Moving it in SolidCraft.** Open the version's `cad/*.sc`, then drive `O_crank` (v1), `O1_crank` (v2/v3) or `W_wheel` (v4, v4a, v4b, v5, v6), or drag the crank or wheel.

## v3: long jaws in +x (three variants). X1, not printed yet — NEEDS PRINT TEST

**Why the v2 pads are cylinders.** The foot plate (g-h-i) turns about 30° while the pads are clamped. A round pad centred on F doesn't care about that, but a rigid jaw does: its tip swings with the foot. For a 10 mm rigid extension the tips would cross into each other by up to 10 mm (`tools/gen_v3.py` explains this). v3 answers this three ways, each built on the v2 base.

**Jaw direction.** "+x" for the jaws is the gripper's own **stroke axis**, the direction the pads travel while clamped, which is 11.6° off the servo's x axis. Only along this axis do the two mirrored jaws stay parallel to each other.

| | v3a pivoting jaws | v3b parallel twin legs | v3c short rigid nose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaw | 25 mm beyond F, Ø11 wide, on top of the stack (z 54.2–62.2) | 91 mm, pinned at both feet, 30 mm overhang past leg B, on top of the stack | the pad grows a 5 mm nose (tip Ø6), recessed 1.7 mm |
| Keeps the faces parallel by | contact: each jaw turns freely on its pin at F and lines up with the item | geometry: leg B = leg A shifted 50 mm and geared to the same crank angle, so the jaw translates without turning | not at all: the nose swings with the foot |
| Gap along the jaw while clamped | 1.53 mm, the same at root and tip | 1.53 mm, the same at root and tip (within 0.004 mm over 80 mm) | nose tips close from 6.0 to 1.3 mm along the stroke; pads stay at 1.53 mm |
| Extra parts | 2 F-hub triangles, 2 jaws | 2 F-hub triangles + 2 extra legs, 2 crank gears, 2 Ø60 idlers, 2 jaws, bigger frame | 2 triangles with a nose |
| CAD stand-in | a parallel relation to a hidden reference (`jaw_ref`) holds the jaws as the item would | exact | exact |
| Main risk | jaws flop when empty; the pivot must allow ~30° during the clamp and 65° over a full turn | size (4 legs, 2 big idlers); the parallelogram jaw rides in a 2 mm slot at leg B so tolerances can't bind it | grips only near the end of the stroke; a longer nose needs more recess and stops gripping (recess needed: 5 mm nose 1.7 mm, 8 mm 5.3 mm, 12 mm 11.1 mm) |

### Hardware on top of v2

- **v3a:** 2 × M3×30 + nyloc as the jaw pivots at F (up through g-h-i, the F hub and the jaw).
- **v3b:** all of v2's leg hardware a second time for the two B legs, plus:
  - 2 × M3×14 + nyloc for the Ø60 idler axles;
  - 2 × M3×12 + thin nut for the crank B axles;
  - 4 × M3×30 + nyloc at the jaw pins: the bore at leg A and the slot at leg B.
- **v3c:** none extra.
