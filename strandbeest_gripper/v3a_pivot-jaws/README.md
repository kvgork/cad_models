## What it is

Each finger's g-h-i triangle carries a Ø11 hub at the foot point F, up to just below the top of the stack. A 25 mm jaw (GRIP-0025, qty 2) turns freely on an M3×30 pin there, above every other part (z 54.2–62.2). On an item the two jaws line up with each other and stay parallel along their length. In CAD a parallel relation to a hidden reference body (`jaw_ref` in the frame) stands in for that contact.

- **Pivot range:** the jaw must be free to turn about 30° on its pin during the clamp, and 65° over a full turn. There are no stops in X1, so the jaws hang free when empty; a light O-ring between jaw and hub can centre them.
- **Parts added to v2:** GRIP-0023/0024 (g-h-i with F hub, finger 1/2) replace the v2 pad triangles GRIP-0020/0022, plus GRIP-0025 (jaw, qty 2).
- **Extra hardware:** 2 × M3×30 + nyloc at the jaw pivots.
