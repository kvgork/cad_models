# storage_drawers v1: Gridfinity drawer in a stackable shelf (STOR-9001)

X1, printed (2026-11-19). **The drawer got stuck in the shelf and the handle had too little grip: use the [v2 drawer](../v2_loose-fit/README.md) (STOR-0005).** The shelf (STOR-0001), spacer (STOR-0003) and test bin (STOR-0004) are unchanged and shared with v2.

A stackable shelf whose top is the lid of its drawer (no floor), a spacer frame for taller drawers, and a drawer with a Gridfinity 5 × 4 floor that hangs on two flanges, clicks shut (detent) and has a soft open stop. It holds the whole iFixit Manta case. Overview: [../README.md](../README.md).
