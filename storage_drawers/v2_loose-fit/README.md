# storage_drawers v2: looser drawer, hook handle (STOR-9002)

X1, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.** A new drawer (STOR-0005) for the **unchanged** v1 shelf, spacer and test bin (STOR-0001, -0003, -0004). The first v1 prints showed two problems (user, 2026-11-19): the drawers got stuck, and the handle gave too little grip. The shelf stays the same (it is the fixed part of the system), so only the drawer changes. **v1 shelves and spacers you have already printed take this drawer as they are.**

![drawer in the shelf](docs/build/step2_drawer-in.png)

## What changed (drawer only)

Every sliding gap between the drawer and the shelf is bigger. The drawer is narrower, its flanges are thinner and reach a bit further out, and the detent bump is taller, so it still reaches the same pocket in the shelf and the click is unchanged.

| Gap (per side) | v1 (STOR-0002) | v2 (STOR-0005) |
|---|---|---|
| Drawer side wall to the detent pad on the shelf wall | 0.5 | **1.0** (wall outer face x 111.5 → 111.0) |
| Flange tip to the shelf wall | 0.5 | **0.7** (flange 3.0 → 3.3 out) |
| Rail edge to the flange's 45° gusset (horizontal) | 0.2 | **0.4** |
| Flange / wall top to the lid underside, seated | 0.6 | **0.8** (flange edge 1.0 → 0.8 thick) |
| Flange land resting on the rail | 1.3 wide | 1.1 wide |
| Detent: bump peak into the pad | 0.6 | 0.6 (bump 1.1 → 1.6 high, peak at the same x 112.6) |
| Manta case to the side wall | 1.5 | 1.0 (inside 219 → 218 wide; front/back 1.5 as before) |

**Hook handle.** v1 had a flat ledge with a 45° underside: your fingers could only pull on friction. v2 has a 100 mm wide trough with a lip that stands **10 mm up at the tip**. Hook your fingertips over the lip and pull on its inner face. Between the lip and the front panel there is a 17 mm wide gap for your fingertips, and the lip's inner top edge has a 1 mm chamfer. It sticks out 23 mm from the drawer front (v1: 15). Its underside is still 45°, so the drawer prints on its floor without supports. The lip top is at z 48.5, below the shelf top (50).
