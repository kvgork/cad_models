# finray_tests v2b: C-socket finger roots (GRIP-9018)

X1, not printed yet. **NEEDS PRINT TEST.** A test of the C-socket root (user request, 2026-11-17), next to v2's pinned bosses. It's the v2 rig with the parallel module only: the holders have two C-shaped sockets, and the fingers have a Ø5 cylinder on each root corner and **slide in from the top**, with no pins.

If you already built v2, print only the two holders (GRIP-0136) and the two fingers (GRIP-0135). They screw onto the same carriages (2 × M3×8 each). The base, carriages, pinion and knob are v2's parts, listed here so the folder is a complete kit.

![done](docs/build/step5_done-top.png)

## What to test

The design review (2026-11-17) expected these problems, which is why v2 uses pinned bosses. This rig shows whether they are real:
- **Wobble.** The cylinders have 0.15 mm play per side in Ø5.3 bores, about ±1 mm at the tip. Grip something and watch the root.
- **Pop-out.** A grip force at mid-finger becomes roughly twice that force on the root cylinders, and one of them is pulled toward its 3.5 mm mouth. Does the TPU squeeze out?
- **Lift-out.** Nothing holds the finger down. Pull the object out upward and sideways (v1 test step 4).
- **The open root.** As in v2, the corners are joined only through the ribs. The sockets hold them 18.2 mm apart and let them turn.

The cylinders stick out 1.6 mm past the grip face behind the root, so the fingers close to a 10 mm gap here (v2: 2 mm).
